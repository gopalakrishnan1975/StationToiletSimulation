# Source review and version history

The original v1–v4 ZIP packages, three PDFs and synthetic results CSV were reviewed from the Station toilet simulation OneDrive folder. v1 introduced a 30-fixture queue demonstration; v2 added utilities and costs; v3 adopted 36 fixtures and welfare; v4 added fare financing. v5 exposed queues in Streamlit and included return walking in boarding feasibility.

Version 6.0.0 supersedes the v5 queue reservations with chronological platform/facility-arrival events, corrects policy/funding consistency, protects against stale results, and adds validated CSV and database/API integration. See docs/DESIGN_AUDIT.md for design findings and docs/generated/VALIDATION_REPORT.md for executed checks.

The historical PDFs have not been rewritten and remain historical source material. docs/TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md is the current technical documentation. Rates and behavioural effects remain illustrative, without real field calibration or current quotations.
